# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Disaster Recovery Requirements for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

Disaster Recovery Requirements establish the principles, procedures, controls, and governance necessary to restore critical platform operations following significant disruptive events while minimizing business interruption, protecting data integrity, and supporting business continuity objectives.

---

# 2. Scope

This document applies to disaster recovery activities involving:

- Application Services
- Databases
- Infrastructure
- Networking
- Cloud Resources
- Configuration Data
- Customer Services
- Operational Services
- Monitoring Systems
- Supporting Enterprise Services

These requirements apply across disaster recovery planning, testing, execution, and post-recovery review activities.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this document are to:

- Restore critical business operations.
- Minimize service disruption.
- Reduce business impact.
- Protect business data.
- Improve operational resilience.
- Support business continuity.
- Improve disaster preparedness.
- Support enterprise governance.
- Improve operational readiness.
- Support long-term platform sustainability.

---

# 4. Disaster Recovery Principles

The QuickBite platform shall follow these principles.

## 4.1 Business Continuity

Recovery activities shall prioritize restoration of critical business operations.

---

## 4.2 Availability

Critical services shall be restored according to approved recovery objectives.

---

## 4.3 Integrity

Recovered systems shall preserve business data integrity.

---

## 4.4 Security

Recovery activities shall maintain organizational security requirements.

---

## 4.5 Governance

Disaster recovery activities shall follow approved operational governance procedures.

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Infrastructure Failure | Hardware or cloud infrastructure outage |

| Network Failure | Network connectivity disruption |
| Application Failure | Critical application service failure |
| Security Incident | Cybersecurity event affecting operations |
| Natural Disaster | Environmental events affecting platform availability |

# 6. Disaster Recovery Standards

Disaster recovery activities shall:

- Follow approved disaster recovery procedures.
- Support complete operational traceability.
- Preserve business data integrity.
- Support recovery validation.
- Follow enterprise security standards.
- Support monitoring and auditing.
- Be periodically tested.
- Follow approved governance processes.

# 21. Acceptance Criteria

The Disaster Recovery Requirements defined in this document shall be considered satisfied when:

- Disaster scenarios are documented and periodically reviewed.
- Recovery Time Objectives (RTO) are defined and validated.
- Recovery Point Objectives (RPO) are defined and supported by backup strategies.
- Recovery sites remain operationally ready.
- Disaster recovery procedures restore critical services successfully.
- Disaster communication plans operate effectively.
- Disaster recovery testing demonstrates recoverability.
- Monitoring and audit requirements are fulfilled.
- Governance procedures are consistently followed.

# 25. Summary

This document establishes the enterprise disaster recovery baseline for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

The requirements defined herein provide comprehensive guidance for disaster scenarios, Recovery Time Objectives (RTO), Recovery Point Objectives (RPO), recovery sites, disaster recovery procedures, communication, testing, monitoring, auditing, and governance.

Collectively, these requirements ensure that the QuickBite platform can recover from major disruptive events in a controlled, secure, auditable, and resilient manner while supporting business continuity, operational excellence, enterprise governance, and long-term platform sustainability.
