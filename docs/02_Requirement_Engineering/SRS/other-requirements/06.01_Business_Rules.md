# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Business Rules for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

Business Rules describe the policies, constraints, and operational decisions that govern how the platform operates. These rules represent business decisions independent of the technical implementation and provide a consistent foundation for product behavior across all platform components.

---

# 2. Scope

These business rules apply to:

- Customers
- Restaurants
- Delivery Partners
- Platform Administrators
- Orders
- Payments
- Promotions
- Refunds
- Ratings and Reviews
- Operational Processes

These rules apply across development, testing, staging, and production environments.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of these Business Rules are to:

- Standardize business operations.
- Ensure consistent platform behavior.
- Improve customer experience.
- Support operational efficiency.
- Reduce ambiguity in business processes.
- Strengthen governance.
- Improve regulatory compliance.
- Support future business expansion.
- Improve maintainability.
- Enable enterprise decision-making.

---

# 4. Business Rule Principles

The QuickBite platform shall follow these principles.

## 4.1 Consistency

Business rules shall be applied consistently across the platform.

---

## 4.2 Transparency

Business decisions shall be traceable and understandable.

---

## 4.3 Fairness

Business policies shall treat stakeholders fairly according to approved organizational guidelines.

---

## 4.4 Flexibility

Business rules shall support controlled future evolution.

---

## 4.5 Governance

Business rule changes shall follow approved change management procedures.

# 25. Summary

This document establishes the enterprise business rule baseline for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

The business rules defined herein provide comprehensive guidance for customer operations, restaurant management, delivery operations, order processing, payments, cancellations, refunds, promotions, loyalty programs, ratings and reviews, administrative activities, and business governance.

Collectively, these rules ensure consistent, auditable, scalable, maintainable, and enterprise-grade business operations while supporting organizational governance, regulatory compliance, operational excellence, and long-term platform evolution.
