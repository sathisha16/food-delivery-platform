# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Maintenance and Support Requirements for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

Maintenance and Support Requirements establish the principles, processes, controls, and governance necessary to maintain platform reliability, resolve operational issues, implement approved improvements, and support business continuity throughout the platform lifecycle.

---

# 2. Scope

This document applies to maintenance and support activities involving:

- Application Services
- Databases
- Infrastructure
- APIs
- Configuration
- Monitoring Systems
- Customer Support
- Operational Support
- Production Incidents
- Platform Enhancements

These requirements apply across development, testing, staging, and production environments.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this document are to:

- Improve platform reliability.
- Reduce operational downtime.
- Support business continuity.
- Improve operational efficiency.
- Enable continuous improvement.
- Improve customer satisfaction.
- Support enterprise governance.
- Improve maintainability.
- Strengthen operational readiness.
- Support long-term platform sustainability.

---

# 4. Maintenance Principles

The QuickBite platform shall follow these principles.

## 4.1 Reliability

Maintenance activities shall preserve platform stability.

---

## 4.2 Availability

Maintenance shall minimize service disruption where practical.

---

## 4.3 Traceability

Maintenance activities shall remain fully traceable.

---

## 4.4 Continuous Improvement

Maintenance processes shall support ongoing platform improvement.

---

## 4.5 Governance

Maintenance activities shall follow approved operational governance procedures.

# 5. Maintenance Classification

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Preventive | Activities that reduce future failures |
| Corrective | Resolution of identified defects |
| Adaptive | Changes required by external factors |
| Perfective | Improvements to functionality, performance, or maintainability |
| Operational Support | Day-to-day operational assistance |
| Incident Response | Production issue resolution |

Maintenance and support activities shall:

- Follow approved operational procedures.
- Support complete operational traceability.
- Preserve platform integrity.
- Support monitoring and reporting.
- Follow enterprise security standards.
- Support auditing.
- Be periodically reviewed.
- Follow approved governance processes.

# 22. Acceptance Criteria

The Maintenance and Support Requirements defined in this document shall be considered satisfied when:

- Preventive maintenance activities are executed according to approved schedules.
- Corrective maintenance restores platform functionality effectively.
- Adaptive maintenance supports evolving business, technology, and regulatory needs.
- Perfective maintenance improves platform quality and maintainability.
- Incident management processes resolve production issues efficiently.
- Support processes operate consistently.
- Service level objectives are monitored and reported.
- Maintenance monitoring and audit requirements are fulfilled.
- Governance procedures are consistently followed.

# 26. Summary

This document establishes the enterprise maintenance and support baseline for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

The requirements defined herein provide comprehensive guidance for preventive, corrective, adaptive, and perfective maintenance, incident management, operational support, service level management, monitoring, auditing, and governance.

Collectively, these requirements ensure that the QuickBite platform remains reliable, maintainable, secure, operationally resilient, and continuously improving throughout its production lifecycle while supporting enterprise governance, operational excellence, and long-term platform sustainability.
