# 1. Purpose

This document defines the assumptions and dependencies for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

Assumptions represent conditions expected to remain true during project execution and platform operation. Dependencies represent external systems, services, organizations, technologies, or operational conditions required for successful delivery and operation of the platform.

---

# 2. Scope

This document includes:

- Business Assumptions
- Technical Assumptions
- Operational Assumptions
- Infrastructure Dependencies
- Third-Party Dependencies
- Organizational Dependencies
- External Dependencies
- Risk Considerations

These assumptions and dependencies apply throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

---

# 25. Summary

This document establishes the enterprise assumptions and dependencies baseline for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

The assumptions and dependencies defined herein provide comprehensive guidance for business assumptions, technical assumptions, operational assumptions, infrastructure dependencies, third-party providers, organizational responsibilities, external dependencies, governance, and risk management.

Collectively, these assumptions and dependencies improve planning accuracy, operational readiness, governance, maintainability, and long-term platform evolution while helping stakeholders understand the boundaries and external factors that influence the success of the QuickBite platform.
