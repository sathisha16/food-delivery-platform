# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Legal and Regulatory Requirements for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

Legal and Regulatory Requirements establish the principles, controls, governance, and compliance obligations necessary to ensure that the platform operates in accordance with applicable laws, regulations, organizational policies, contractual obligations, and industry standards.

---

# 2. Scope

This document applies to:

- Customer Data
- Restaurant Data
- Delivery Partner Data
- Payment Information
- Operational Records
- Platform Software
- APIs
- Third-Party Integrations
- Audit Information
- Compliance Documentation

These requirements apply across development, testing, staging, production, and operational support activities.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of this document are to:

- Support legal compliance.
- Protect customer information.
- Improve regulatory readiness.
- Support enterprise governance.
- Improve auditability.
- Protect intellectual property.
- Support contractual compliance.
- Improve operational transparency.
- Reduce compliance risks.
- Support long-term platform sustainability.

# 23. Acceptance Criteria

- Privacy requirements are implemented and followed.

- Record retention policies are implemented.

- Third-party compliance obligations are managed.
- Regulatory changes are assessed and implemented appropriately.
- Governance procedures are consistently followed.

# 27. Summary

This document establishes the enterprise legal and regulatory compliance baseline for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

The requirements defined herein provide comprehensive guidance for legal compliance, privacy, data protection, intellectual property, software licensing, contractual obligations, record retention, compliance monitoring, regulatory change management, risk management, training, auditing, and governance.

Collectively, these requirements ensure that the QuickBite platform operates in a legally compliant, secure, auditable, and well-governed manner while supporting organizational accountability, regulatory readiness, enterprise governance, and long-term platform sustainability.
