# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Notification Interface Requirements for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

Notification Interface Requirements specify how the QuickBite platform delivers transactional, operational, and informational notifications to customers, restaurants, delivery partners, administrators, and supporting enterprise systems.

These requirements establish standardized notification practices that ensure secure, reliable, timely, auditable, scalable, and maintainable communication across supported notification channels.

---

# 2. Scope

These requirements apply to all notification mechanisms supported by the QuickBite platform, including:

- Email Notifications
- SMS Notifications
- Push Notifications
- In-App Notifications
- Order Notifications
- Payment Notifications
- Promotional Notifications
- Administrative Notifications
- Notification Templates
- Notification Delivery Tracking

These requirements apply across development, testing, staging, disaster recovery, and production environments.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of these Notification Interface Requirements are to:

- Standardize notification delivery.
- Improve communication reliability.
- Support multiple notification channels.
- Improve customer engagement.
- Reduce notification failures.
- Support enterprise scalability.
- Improve operational visibility.
- Enable notification governance.
- Support future notification providers.
- Maintain enterprise communication standards.

---

# 4. Notification Principles

The QuickBite platform shall follow these principles.

## 4.1 Reliability

Notifications shall be delivered reliably through approved channels.

---

## 4.2 Timeliness

Notifications shall be delivered within acceptable operational timeframes.

---

## 4.3 Security

Notification content shall protect sensitive information.

---

## 4.4 Maintainability

Notification services shall remain modular, configurable, and maintainable.

---

## 4.5 Observability

Notification processing shall support monitoring, diagnostics, and auditing.

# 25. Summary

This document establishes the enterprise notification interface baseline for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

The requirements defined herein provide comprehensive guidance for email, SMS, push notifications, in-app notifications, notification templates, delivery tracking, retry mechanisms, user notification preferences, notification security, monitoring, and auditing.

Collectively, these requirements ensure reliable, secure, scalable, auditable, and maintainable notification services while supporting enterprise communication standards, cloud-native architecture, and long-term platform evolution.
