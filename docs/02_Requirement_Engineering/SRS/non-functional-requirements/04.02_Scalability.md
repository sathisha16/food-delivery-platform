# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Scalability Requirements for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

Scalability Requirements establish the engineering principles and measurable objectives that enable the platform to efficiently support increasing workloads, growing business demand, expanding geographic coverage, and continuous product evolution without significant degradation in system performance, reliability, or maintainability.

These requirements guide architectural design, infrastructure planning, software development, deployment strategies, and operational decision-making throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

---

# 2. Scope

These Scalability Requirements apply to all production components of the QuickBite platform, including but not limited to:

- Customer Web Application
- Customer Mobile Application
- Restaurant Portal
- Delivery Partner Application
- Administrator Portal
- API Gateway
- Authentication Service
- Customer Service
- Restaurant Service
- Menu Service
- Shopping Cart Service
- Checkout Service
- Payment Service
- Order Service
- Delivery Service
- Notification Service
- Search Service
- Database Layer
- Cache Layer
- Message Broker
- Object Storage
- Monitoring Platform
- CI/CD Infrastructure

The scalability objectives defined in this document apply to synchronous services, asynchronous event-driven processing, scheduled background jobs, distributed microservices, and supporting cloud infrastructure.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of these Scalability Requirements are to ensure that the QuickBite platform:

- Supports continuous business growth.
- Handles increasing customer traffic.
- Supports horizontal scaling.
- Minimizes operational bottlenecks.
- Maintains predictable performance during peak demand.
- Enables independent scaling of microservices.
- Supports cloud-native deployment models.
- Supports multi-region expansion.
- Enables cost-efficient infrastructure utilization.
- Provides a scalable foundation for future product features.

---

# 4. Scalability Principles

The QuickBite platform shall be designed according to the following engineering principles.

## 4.1 Horizontal First

Application scalability shall primarily be achieved through horizontal scaling rather than vertical hardware upgrades.

---

## 4.2 Stateless Services

Business services should remain stateless wherever practical to simplify scaling and load balancing.

---

## 4.3 Independent Service Scaling

Each microservice shall be independently deployable and independently scalable based on workload characteristics.

---

## 4.4 Elastic Infrastructure

Infrastructure shall automatically adapt to changing workloads whenever supported by the deployment environment.

---

## 4.5 Performance Isolation

Heavy workloads generated by one business domain shall not significantly impact unrelated platform services.
