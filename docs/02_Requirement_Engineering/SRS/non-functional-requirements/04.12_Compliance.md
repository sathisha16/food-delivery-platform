# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Compliance Requirements for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

Compliance Requirements establish the legal, regulatory, security, privacy, financial, operational, and organizational obligations that the QuickBite platform shall satisfy throughout its Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) and operational lifecycle.

As an enterprise-scale food delivery platform processing customer information, restaurant data, payment transactions, and operational records, QuickBite shall comply with applicable laws, industry standards, organizational policies, contractual obligations, and engineering governance practices.

These requirements support enterprise risk management, information security, privacy protection, financial integrity, audit readiness, and continuous regulatory compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

These Compliance Requirements apply to all components of the QuickBite platform, including:

- Customer Applications
- Restaurant Portal
- Delivery Partner Application
- Administrator Portal
- Backend Services
- REST APIs
- Databases
- Authentication Services
- Payment Services
- Notification Services
- Analytics Services
- Logging Infrastructure
- Monitoring Systems
- Cloud Infrastructure
- CI/CD Pipelines

These requirements apply across development, testing, staging, disaster recovery, and production environments.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of these Compliance Requirements are to ensure that the QuickBite platform:

- Complies with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.
- Protects customer and business data.
- Supports financial compliance.
- Maintains audit readiness.
- Enforces engineering governance.
- Protects operational integrity.
- Supports secure software development.
- Enables regulatory reporting.
- Supports continuous compliance monitoring.
- Reduces organizational risk.
