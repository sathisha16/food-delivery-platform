# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Reliability Requirements for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

Reliability Requirements establish the engineering principles, operational expectations, and measurable objectives that ensure the QuickBite platform consistently performs its intended functions correctly over time, even in the presence of software defects, infrastructure failures, network disruptions, unexpected workloads, and external service dependencies.

A reliable food delivery platform must ensure that customer orders are processed correctly, payments are handled safely, notifications are delivered accurately, delivery tracking remains dependable, and business operations continue without data corruption or inconsistent system behavior.

These requirements guide software architecture, application development, infrastructure design, operational procedures, monitoring, testing, and continuous improvement throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of these Reliability Requirements are to ensure that the QuickBite platform:

- Performs business operations correctly.
- Maintains consistent system behavior.
- Prevents unnecessary service failures.
- Handles operational faults gracefully.
- Preserves business data integrity.
- Supports resilient distributed systems.
- Minimizes operational risk.
- Enables predictable platform behavior.
- Supports continuous business operations.
- Improves long-term software quality.

---

# 4. Reliability Principles

The QuickBite platform shall follow these engineering principles.

## 4.1 Reliability by Design

Reliability shall be considered throughout architecture, development, testing, deployment, and production operations.

---

## 4.2 Fail Gracefully

Unexpected failures shall degrade system functionality in a controlled manner rather than causing complete platform outages.

---

## 4.3 Defensive Engineering

Applications shall validate inputs, handle exceptions appropriately, and prevent unnecessary system failures.

---

## 4.4 Operational Resilience

Reliable systems shall tolerate recoverable failures while maintaining critical business operations.

---

## 4.5 Continuous Verification

Reliability shall be continuously evaluated using monitoring, testing, operational metrics, and incident analysis.

# 25. Summary

This document establishes the enterprise reliability baseline for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

The requirements defined herein provide measurable engineering objectives for fault tolerance, data consistency, resilient distributed systems, reliable messaging, operational recovery, error handling, and continuous reliability improvement.

Collectively, these requirements ensure that the QuickBite platform consistently performs critical business operations correctly, maintains business data integrity, tolerates recoverable failures, and delivers dependable services expected from a modern enterprise-scale food delivery platform.
