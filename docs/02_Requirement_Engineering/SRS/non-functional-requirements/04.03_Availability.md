# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Availability Requirements for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

Availability Requirements specify the engineering objectives, operational practices, architectural principles, and measurable criteria that ensure QuickBite services remain continuously accessible to customers, restaurants, delivery partners, administrators, and integrated external systems.

High availability is critical for food delivery platforms because customers expect uninterrupted ordering, restaurants require continuous order processing, delivery partners depend on real-time updates, and business operations must continue even during infrastructure failures or maintenance activities.

These requirements establish the minimum availability expectations that shall guide architecture design, software development, infrastructure deployment, production operations, disaster preparedness, and continuous improvement throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of these Availability Requirements are to ensure that the QuickBite platform:

- Provides uninterrupted service whenever possible.
- Minimizes planned downtime.
- Minimizes unplanned downtime.
- Supports fault-tolerant architectures.
- Detects failures rapidly.
- Recovers automatically wherever practical.
- Maintains business continuity.
- Supports rolling deployments.
- Supports zero-downtime upgrades where feasible.
- Delivers a highly available customer experience.

---

# 4. Availability Principles

The QuickBite platform shall follow these availability engineering principles.

## 4.1 High Availability by Design

Availability shall be considered during architecture, development, testing, deployment, and production operations.

---

## 4.2 Eliminate Single Points of Failure

Critical platform components shall avoid single points of failure wherever practical.

---

## 4.3 Fault Isolation

Failures occurring within one subsystem shall not unnecessarily impact unrelated services.

---

## 4.4 Automatic Recovery

Systems shall automatically recover from recoverable failures whenever technically feasible.

---

## 4.5 Continuous Monitoring

Availability shall be continuously monitored using centralized operational monitoring systems.

# 25. Summary

This document establishes the enterprise availability baseline for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

The requirements defined herein provide measurable engineering objectives for high availability, fault tolerance, redundancy, disaster recovery, business continuity, operational monitoring, and production resilience.

Collectively, these requirements ensure that the QuickBite platform remains continuously accessible, resilient to failures, and capable of delivering reliable services expected from a modern enterprise-scale food delivery platform.
