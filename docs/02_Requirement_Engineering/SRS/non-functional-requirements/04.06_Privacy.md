# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Privacy Requirements for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

Privacy Requirements establish the engineering principles, organizational controls, and operational practices necessary to ensure that personal data is collected, processed, stored, shared, retained, and deleted in a lawful, transparent, secure, and privacy-preserving manner.

As an enterprise-scale food delivery platform, QuickBite processes personally identifiable information (PII), customer addresses, contact information, location data, payment-related information, restaurant business information, delivery partner information, and operational data. Therefore, privacy shall be integrated throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), business processes, application architecture, infrastructure, and operational procedures.

These requirements support Privacy by Design, Privacy by Default, responsible data governance, regulatory readiness, and customer trust while enabling secure business operations.

---

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of these Privacy Requirements are to ensure that the QuickBite platform:

- Protects customer privacy.
- Minimizes unnecessary personal data collection.
- Supports transparent data processing.
- Enables customer control over personal information.
- Supports regulatory compliance.
- Reduces privacy risks.
- Protects sensitive personal information.
- Supports responsible data governance.
- Preserves customer trust.
- Enables Privacy by Design.

---

# 4. Privacy Principles

The QuickBite platform shall follow these engineering principles.

## 4.1 Privacy by Design

Privacy shall be incorporated throughout architecture, software development, infrastructure design, deployment, and operational processes.

---

## 4.2 Privacy by Default

Only the minimum personal information required for business operations shall be collected and processed by default.

---

## 4.3 Data Minimization

Personal information shall be limited to information necessary for legitimate business purposes.

---

## 4.4 Transparency

Customers shall be informed regarding the collection, processing, storage, and sharing of their personal information.

---

## 4.5 Accountability

Privacy responsibilities shall be clearly defined, monitored, and periodically reviewed throughout the organization.
