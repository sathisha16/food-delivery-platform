# 1. Purpose

This document defines the Security Requirements for the QuickBite Food Delivery Platform.

Security Requirements establish the engineering principles, operational controls, and technical safeguards necessary to protect customer information, business data, payment transactions, application services, infrastructure resources, and third-party integrations against unauthorized access, misuse, cyber threats, and operational risks.

As an enterprise-scale food delivery platform, QuickBite processes sensitive customer information, payment transactions, restaurant business data, delivery operations, and administrative functions. Therefore, security shall be integrated throughout architecture, software development, infrastructure design, deployment, monitoring, and operational processes.

These requirements guide secure software development, infrastructure protection, identity management, application security, operational security, compliance, and continuous security improvement throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

# 3. Objectives

The objectives of these Security Requirements are to ensure that the QuickBite platform:

- Protects customer information.
- Protects payment transactions.
- Prevents unauthorized access.
- Maintains data confidentiality.
- Preserves data integrity.
- Ensures service availability.
- Supports secure software development.
- Detects security threats rapidly.
- Responds effectively to security incidents.
- Continuously improves platform security.

# 4. Security Principles

The QuickBite platform shall follow these engineering principles.

## 4.1 Security by Design

Security shall be incorporated throughout architecture, software development, testing, deployment, and production operations.

---

## 4.2 Least Privilege

Users, services, and administrators shall receive only the minimum permissions necessary to perform their responsibilities.

---

## 4.3 Defense in Depth

Multiple independent security controls shall protect critical business assets.

---

## 4.4 Zero Trust

All requests shall be authenticated, authorized, and validated regardless of network location.

---

## 4.5 Continuous Security

Security shall be continuously monitored, assessed, and improved throughout the platform lifecycle.
