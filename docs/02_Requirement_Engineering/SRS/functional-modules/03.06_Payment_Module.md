## 3.6 Payment Module

The Payment Module enables customers to securely complete payments using supported payment methods, process transactions, handle payment failures, initiate refunds, and maintain payment history.

The functional requirements included in the Payment Module are:

### 3.6.1 Initiate Payment

#### 3.6.1.1 Description

The Initiate Payment use case enables the customer to begin the payment process after successfully completing the checkout process. The system validates the order, verifies payment eligibility, creates a secure payment session, prepares the payment request, and initiates communication with the configured payment service. The payment initiation process ensures that only valid, unpaid, active orders proceed to payment while preventing duplicate payment attempts, unauthorized access, and invalid payment requests. This use case acts as the entry point for all supported payment methods including online payments and Cash on Delivery (COD).
