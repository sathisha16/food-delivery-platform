## 3.8 Delivery Management Module

The Delivery Management Module enables efficient assignment, execution, monitoring, and completion of food deliveries from the restaurant to the customer. It supports intelligent delivery partner assignment, pickup verification, live delivery tracking, delivery status management, customer communication, proof of delivery, earnings calculation, operational monitoring, and complete auditability of the delivery lifecycle.

The functional requirements included in the Delivery Management Module are:

### 3.8.1 Assign Delivery Partner

#### 3.8.1.1 Description

The Assign Delivery Partner use case enables the system to automatically assign the most suitable delivery partner to a confirmed customer order. The assignment process considers partner availability, serviceability, current location, delivery capacity, workload, estimated pickup time, business rules, and operational constraints to ensure efficient and timely order fulfillment while maximizing delivery performance.

### 3.8.26 Update Delivery Partner Availability

#### 3.8.26.1 Description

The Update Delivery Partner Availability use case enables the system to maintain the real-time availability status of delivery partners for order assignment and delivery operations. Delivery partners may update their availability manually or the system may automatically update their status based on delivery lifecycle events, shift schedules, login status, location availability, policy rules, or operational conditions. The system validates the requested status transition, updates the partner's availability, synchronizes the updated status across authorized services, and maintains complete traceability and auditability while ensuring operational efficiency, accurate delivery partner allocation, and regulatory compliance.
