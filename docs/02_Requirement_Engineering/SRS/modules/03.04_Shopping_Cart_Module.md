## 3.4 Shopping Cart Module

The Shopping Cart Module enables customers to add menu items, modify cart contents, review selected items, apply discounts, and calculate the total payable amount before proceeding to checkout.

The functional requirements included in the Shopping Cart Module are:

### 3.4.1 Add Item to Cart

#### 3.4.1.1 Description

The Add Item to Cart functionality enables a customer to add one or more menu items, including selected variants, customizations, and quantities, from a restaurant menu to the shopping cart. The system shall validate item availability, restaurant eligibility, customization options, pricing, and applicable business rules before successfully adding the selected item to the shopping cart.

#### 3.4.1.2 Actors

**Primary Actor**

- Customer

**Supporting Actors**

- Shopping Cart Service
- Menu Management Service
- Restaurant Management Service
- Inventory Management Service
- Pricing Service
- Promotion Service
- Authentication Service
- Audit Logging Service

#### 3.4.1.3 Preconditions

The following preconditions shall be satisfied before a customer can add an item to the shopping cart:

1. The customer shall have access to the QuickBite platform.

2. The selected restaurant shall exist and be active.

3. The selected menu item shall exist.

4. The selected menu item shall be available for ordering.

5. The selected restaurant shall currently accept customer orders.

6. The customer shall have selected a valid quantity.

7. Any mandatory item variants or customizations shall be selected before adding the item to the cart.

8. The shopping cart shall be available for modification.

9. The pricing information for the selected item shall be successfully retrieved.

10. The system shall successfully validate applicable business rules before adding the item to the cart.

#### 3.4.1.4 Trigger

The Add Item to Cart functionality shall be initiated when the customer selects a menu item and clicks or taps the **"Add to Cart"** button from the restaurant menu, food item details page, recommended items section, favorite items, search results, or any other supported menu listing within the QuickBite platform.

---

#### 3.4.1.5 Input Fields

| Field Name | Description | Mandatory |
|------------|-------------|-----------|
| Restaurant ID | Unique identifier of the selected restaurant. | Yes |
| Menu Item ID | Unique identifier of the selected menu item. | Yes |
| Quantity | Number of units the customer wishes to add to the cart. | Yes |
| Item Variant | Selected item variant (e.g., Size, Portion, Flavor), where applicable. | Conditional |
| Item Customizations | Selected customizations such as extra toppings, ingredient removal, spice level, or cooking preferences. | Conditional |
| Special Instructions | Additional preparation instructions provided by the customer. | Optional |

---

#### 3.4.1.6 Business Rules

1. The system shall allow customers to add only menu items that are currently available for ordering.

2. The customer shall add menu items from only one restaurant into a shopping cart at a time.

3. If the customer attempts to add an item from a different restaurant while the cart already contains items, the system shall request customer confirmation before replacing the existing cart contents.

4. Mandatory item variants and required customizations shall be selected before the item can be added to the shopping cart.

5. The quantity of each cart item shall be greater than zero and shall not exceed the maximum quantity permitted by the restaurant.

6. The system shall calculate the item price based on the selected quantity, variants, customizations, and current pricing rules.

7. Restaurant-specific pricing, taxes, promotional offers, and availability rules shall be applied during cart creation.

8. The system shall validate that the selected menu item remains available before successfully adding it to the shopping cart.

9. Duplicate cart items having identical variants and customizations may be merged by increasing the quantity instead of creating separate cart entries.

10. Cart information shall be securely stored for authenticated customers and maintained according to platform cart retention policies.

11. Every successful Add Item to Cart operation shall be recorded for auditing, analytics, customer behavior analysis, and operational monitoring purposes.

12. Adding an item to the shopping cart shall not reserve restaurant inventory until the customer successfully places the order.

#### 3.4.1.7 Validations

The system shall perform the following validations before adding the selected menu item to the shopping cart:

1. The selected restaurant shall exist and be active.

2. The selected restaurant shall currently accept customer orders.

3. The selected menu item shall exist.

4. The selected menu item shall be active, published, and available for ordering.

5. The selected quantity shall be greater than zero.

6. The selected quantity shall not exceed the maximum quantity permitted for the menu item.

7. All mandatory item variants shall be selected.

8. All mandatory customization options shall be completed before adding the item to the shopping cart.

9. The selected variants and customizations shall be valid for the selected menu item.

10. The latest item price shall be successfully retrieved before calculating the cart amount.

11. If the shopping cart already contains items from another restaurant, the system shall request customer confirmation before replacing the existing cart contents.

12. The customer shall have permission to modify the shopping cart.

13. The shopping cart shall be available and capable of accepting additional items.

14. The system shall verify that the requested item has not become unavailable during the add-to-cart process.

15. The system shall reject duplicate or invalid customization values that are not supported by the selected menu item.

16. All mandatory business rules and pricing validations shall be successfully completed before the item is added to the shopping cart.

---

#### 3.4.1.8 Main Flow

1. The customer browses the restaurant menu and selects a menu item.

2. The system displays the available variants, customizations, price, and quantity selection options for the selected menu item.

3. The customer selects the desired quantity, variants, customizations, and optional special instructions.

4. The customer initiates the **Add to Cart** action.

5. The system validates the restaurant, menu item, quantity, variants, customizations, pricing information, and applicable business rules.

6. The system verifies whether the shopping cart already contains items from a different restaurant.

7. If the shopping cart contains items from another restaurant, the system requests customer confirmation before replacing the existing cart contents.

8. Upon successful validation, the system adds the selected menu item to the shopping cart.

9. If an identical cart item already exists with the same variants and customizations, the system updates the existing cart item quantity instead of creating a duplicate entry.

10. The system recalculates the shopping cart totals, applicable taxes, fees, discounts, and promotional benefits.

11. The system updates the shopping cart and securely stores the latest cart information.

12. The system records the Add Item to Cart activity for auditing, analytics, customer behavior analysis, and operational monitoring purposes.

13. The system displays the updated shopping cart summary and confirms that the selected item has been successfully added.

#### 3.4.1.9 Alternate Flows

##### AF-1: Mandatory Variant Not Selected

1. The customer attempts to add the selected menu item without choosing a mandatory variant.

2. The system detects the missing mandatory variant selection.

3. The system displays an appropriate validation message requesting the customer to select the required variant.

4. The shopping cart shall not be updated until all mandatory variants are selected.

---

##### AF-2: Mandatory Customization Not Selected

1. The customer submits the Add to Cart request without selecting required customization options.

2. The system validates the customization requirements.

3. The system prompts the customer to complete all mandatory customizations.

4. The selected menu item shall not be added to the shopping cart until the required customizations are provided.

---

##### AF-3: Menu Item Becomes Unavailable

1. The customer initiates the Add to Cart request.

2. Before completing the request, the system determines that the selected menu item is no longer available.

3. The system informs the customer that the selected menu item is currently unavailable.

4. The system shall not add the unavailable item to the shopping cart.

---

##### AF-4: Restaurant Changed

1. The shopping cart already contains items from Restaurant A.

2. The customer attempts to add a menu item from Restaurant B.

3. The system informs the customer that the shopping cart currently contains items from another restaurant.

4. The system requests confirmation to clear the existing shopping cart.

5. If the customer confirms, the system removes the existing cart items and adds the newly selected menu item.

6. If the customer declines, the existing shopping cart shall remain unchanged.

---

##### AF-5: Duplicate Cart Item

1. The customer adds a menu item that already exists in the shopping cart with identical variants and customizations.

2. The system identifies the duplicate cart entry.

3. The system increases the quantity of the existing cart item instead of creating a new cart entry.

4. The shopping cart totals shall be recalculated.

---

##### AF-6: Maximum Quantity Exceeded

1. The customer selects a quantity exceeding the maximum quantity permitted for the menu item.

2. The system validates the requested quantity.

3. The system displays an appropriate validation message indicating the permitted quantity limit.

4. The requested item shall not be added until a valid quantity is selected.

---

##### AF-7: Pricing Changed

1. The system detects that the latest menu item price differs from the price displayed to the customer.

2. The system retrieves the latest pricing information.

3. The system updates the displayed price.

4. The customer shall be informed of the revised price before the item is added to the shopping cart.

---

##### AF-8: System Failure

1. An unexpected application, network, or service failure occurs during the Add Item to Cart operation.

2. The system aborts the current request.

3. The system displays an appropriate error message.

4. No incomplete or partially processed cart data shall be stored.

5. The customer may retry the Add Item to Cart operation after the issue is resolved.

#### 3.4.1.10 Postconditions

Upon completion of the Add Item to Cart functionality, one of the following postconditions shall apply:

##### Successful Completion

1. The selected menu item shall be successfully added to the customer's shopping cart.

2. If an identical cart item already exists with the same variants and customizations, the system shall update the existing cart item quantity instead of creating a duplicate cart entry.

3. The shopping cart shall reflect the latest selected quantity, variants, customizations, pricing, and special instructions.

4. The system shall recalculate the shopping cart totals, including item subtotal, applicable taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, discounts, promotional offers, and grand total, where applicable.

5. The updated shopping cart shall be securely persisted according to platform cart retention policies.

6. The customer shall be able to continue shopping or proceed to checkout using the updated shopping cart.

7. The Add Item to Cart activity shall be recorded for auditing, analytics, customer behavior analysis, reporting, and operational monitoring purposes.

8. Adding an item to the shopping cart shall not reserve restaurant inventory or create an order.

9. No payment transaction shall be initiated as a result of adding an item to the shopping cart.

##### Unsuccessful Completion

1. The selected menu item shall not be added to the shopping cart.

2. Existing shopping cart contents shall remain unchanged.

3. The system shall notify the customer of the reason for the failure.

4. No incomplete or partially processed shopping cart information shall be stored.

5. No pricing, inventory, customer profile, restaurant information, or order data shall be modified due to the failed operation.

6. The customer's authenticated session shall remain active unless termination is required by platform security policies.

---

#### 3.4.1.11 Success Response

Upon successful completion of the Add Item to Cart functionality, the system shall:

1. Confirm that the selected menu item has been successfully added to the shopping cart.

2. Display the updated shopping cart summary.

3. Display the updated cart item count.

4. Display the latest item subtotal, applicable taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, discounts, promotional offers, and grand total, where applicable.

5. Reflect the selected quantity, variants, customizations, and special instructions in the shopping cart.

6. Persist the updated shopping cart for future retrieval according to platform policies.

7. Allow the customer to continue shopping or proceed to checkout.

8. Record the successful Add Item to Cart activity for auditing, analytics, customer behavior analysis, reporting, and operational monitoring purposes.

9. Ensure that no order, payment transaction, inventory reservation, or restaurant fulfillment process is initiated until the customer proceeds to checkout and successfully places the order.

---

#### 3.4.1.12 Failure Response

If the Add Item to Cart functionality cannot be completed successfully, the system shall:

1. Inform the customer that the selected menu item could not be added to the shopping cart.

2. Display an appropriate and user-friendly error message indicating the reason for the failure, where applicable.

3. Preserve the existing shopping cart contents without modification.

4. Prevent creation of duplicate, incomplete, or inconsistent shopping cart entries.

5. Allow the customer to retry the Add Item to Cart operation when the failure is recoverable.

6. Record the failed Add Item to Cart activity for diagnostics, auditing, analytics, security monitoring, and operational reporting purposes, where applicable.

7. Ensure that no order, payment transaction, inventory reservation, restaurant fulfillment activity, or customer profile modification occurs as a result of the failed request.

8. Maintain the customer's authenticated session and current application state unless termination is required by platform security or system policies.

---

The Add Item to Cart functionality shall be considered successfully implemented when all the following conditions are satisfied:

|-------|----------------------|

| AC-AIC-002 | The system shall support item quantities, variants, customizations, and special instructions. |
| AC-AIC-003 | Mandatory variants and customizations shall be validated before the item is added to the shopping cart. |
| AC-AIC-004 | The system shall prevent customers from adding unavailable or inactive menu items. |
| AC-AIC-005 | The system shall support only one restaurant per shopping cart at any given time. |
| AC-AIC-006 | When items from another restaurant are selected, the system shall request customer confirmation before replacing the existing shopping cart contents. |
| AC-AIC-007 | Duplicate cart items with identical variants and customizations shall be merged by updating the quantity. |
| AC-AIC-008 | The system shall accurately calculate the latest shopping cart totals after every successful Add Item to Cart operation. |
| AC-AIC-009 | The updated shopping cart shall be securely persisted according to platform policies. |

### 3.4.2 View Shopping Cart

#### 3.4.2.1 Description

The View Shopping Cart functionality enables a customer to view all items currently added to the shopping cart before proceeding to checkout. The system shall display complete cart details, including selected menu items, quantities, variants, customizations, pricing, applicable taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, discounts, promotional offers, estimated delivery time, and the grand total.

#### 3.4.2.2 Actors

**Primary Actor**

- Customer

**Supporting Actors**

- Shopping Cart Service
- Pricing Service
- Promotion Service
- Restaurant Management Service
- Menu Management Service
- Tax Calculation Service
- Authentication Service
- Audit Logging Service

#### 3.4.2.3 Preconditions

The following preconditions shall be satisfied before a customer can view the shopping cart:

1. The customer shall have access to the QuickBite platform.

2. A shopping cart shall exist for the customer, whether empty or containing one or more items.

3. The shopping cart shall be successfully retrieved from the system.

4. The latest pricing information shall be available.

5. Applicable taxes, charges, discounts, and promotional offers shall be retrievable.

6. Restaurant information associated with the shopping cart shall be available.

7. The customer shall have permission to access the shopping cart.

8. The system shall successfully retrieve the latest shopping cart information before displaying the cart.

#### 3.4.2.4 Trigger

The View Shopping Cart functionality shall be initiated when the customer selects the **Shopping Cart** icon, **View Cart** button, cart summary section, or any other supported navigation option within the QuickBite platform.

#### 3.4.2.5 Input Fields

| Field Name | Description | Mandatory |
|------------|-------------|-----------|
| Customer ID | Unique identifier of the customer. | Yes |
| Shopping Cart ID | Unique identifier of the customer's shopping cart. | Yes |

#### 3.4.2.6 Business Rules

1. The system shall display the latest shopping cart information available at the time of retrieval.

2. The shopping cart shall contain menu items from only one restaurant at any given time.

3. The system shall display each cart item along with its selected quantity, variants, customizations, special instructions, unit price, and item subtotal.

4. The system shall calculate and display the latest item subtotal, applicable taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, discounts, promotional offers, and grand total.

5. The system shall retrieve and display the latest pricing information before presenting the shopping cart.

6. The system shall automatically apply eligible promotional offers and discounts according to platform business policies.

7. The system shall display the estimated delivery time based on the current restaurant operations and delivery availability.

8. The system shall indicate if any cart item becomes unavailable, inactive, or temporarily unavailable after it was added to the shopping cart.

9. The system shall display applicable warnings whenever the shopping cart does not satisfy minimum order value requirements.

10. Viewing the shopping cart shall not modify any cart contents, pricing information, customer profile, restaurant information, inventory data, or order information.

11. Every successful shopping cart retrieval shall be recorded for auditing, analytics, customer behavior analysis, and operational monitoring purposes.

12. The shopping cart information shall be displayed only to the authorized customer who owns the shopping cart.

---

#### 3.4.2.7 Validations

The system shall perform the following validations before displaying the shopping cart:

1. The customer shall be authorized to access the requested shopping cart.

2. The shopping cart shall exist.

3. The shopping cart information shall be successfully retrieved.

4. The associated restaurant shall exist and be active.

5. The latest menu item information shall be successfully retrieved.

6. The latest pricing information shall be available.

7. Applicable taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, discounts, and promotional offers shall be successfully calculated.

8. The system shall verify whether any cart item has become unavailable, inactive, or out of stock.

9. The system shall validate the minimum order value requirements, where applicable.

10. The estimated delivery time shall be successfully calculated before displaying the shopping cart.

11. The customer shall have permission to access only their own shopping cart.

12. All mandatory business rules shall be successfully validated before displaying the shopping cart.

---

#### 3.4.2.8 Main Flow

1. The customer initiates the **View Shopping Cart** request.

2. The system authenticates the customer, where applicable.

3. The system retrieves the customer's latest shopping cart.

4. The system retrieves the associated restaurant information.

5. The system retrieves the latest menu item details and pricing information.

6. The system validates the availability of all cart items.

7. The system calculates the item subtotal, applicable taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, discounts, promotional offers, and grand total.

8. The system calculates the estimated delivery time.

9. The system prepares the complete shopping cart summary.

10. The system displays all shopping cart items, selected quantities, variants, customizations, special instructions, pricing breakdown, estimated delivery time, and grand total.

11. The system highlights any unavailable items, pricing changes, minimum order value warnings, or other applicable notifications.

12. The system records the View Shopping Cart activity for auditing, analytics, customer behavior analysis, and operational monitoring purposes.

#### 3.4.2.9 Alternate Flows

##### AF-1: Empty Shopping Cart

2. The system determines that the shopping cart contains no items.

3. The system displays an empty shopping cart message.

4. The system provides options to browse restaurants or continue shopping.

---

### 3.4.3 Update Item Quantity

#### 3.4.3.1 Description

The Update Item Quantity functionality enables a customer to increase or decrease the quantity of an existing menu item in the shopping cart. The system shall validate quantity limits, item availability, pricing, applicable business rules, and recalculate the shopping cart totals after every successful quantity update.

#### 3.4.3.2 Actors

**Primary Actor**

- Customer

**Supporting Actors**

- Shopping Cart Service
- Menu Management Service
- Inventory Management Service
- Pricing Service
- Promotion Service
- Restaurant Management Service
- Authentication Service
- Audit Logging Service

#### 3.4.3.3 Preconditions

The following preconditions shall be satisfied before a customer can update the quantity of a shopping cart item:

1. The customer shall have access to the QuickBite platform.

2. A shopping cart shall exist.

3. The shopping cart shall contain at least one menu item.

4. The selected shopping cart item shall exist.

5. The customer shall have permission to modify the shopping cart.

6. The selected menu item shall remain available for ordering.

7. The requested quantity shall comply with restaurant quantity restrictions.

8. The latest pricing information shall be successfully retrievable before updating the shopping cart.

#### 3.4.3.4 Trigger

The Update Item Quantity functionality shall be initiated when the customer increases or decreases the quantity of a shopping cart item using the quantity controls or directly updates the quantity value within the shopping cart.

#### 3.4.3.5 Input Fields

| Field Name | Description | Mandatory |
|------------|-------------|-----------|
| Customer ID | Unique identifier of the customer. | Yes |
| Shopping Cart ID | Unique identifier of the shopping cart. | Yes |
| Cart Item ID | Unique identifier of the shopping cart item. | Yes |
| Updated Quantity | Revised quantity requested by the customer. | Yes |

#### 3.4.3.6 Business Rules

1. The system shall allow customers to update the quantity only for items currently present in the shopping cart.

2. The updated quantity shall be greater than zero and shall not exceed the maximum quantity permitted by the restaurant.

3. The system shall validate the latest availability of the selected menu item before updating the quantity.

4. The system shall recalculate the item subtotal based on the updated quantity.

5. The system shall automatically recalculate applicable taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, discounts, promotional offers, and the grand total after every successful quantity update.

6. The system shall re-evaluate all applicable promotional offers and discount eligibility whenever the item quantity changes.

7. If the updated quantity causes the shopping cart to no longer satisfy the minimum order value, the system shall notify the customer.

8. Updating the quantity of a shopping cart item shall not modify the selected variants, customizations, or special instructions associated with that item.

9. The updated shopping cart shall be securely persisted according to platform cart retention policies.

10. Every successful Update Item Quantity operation shall be recorded for auditing, analytics, customer behavior analysis, and operational monitoring purposes.

11. Updating the quantity of a shopping cart item shall not reserve restaurant inventory or create an order.

12. No payment transaction shall be initiated as a result of updating the item quantity.

---

#### 3.4.3.7 Validations

The system shall perform the following validations before updating the quantity of a shopping cart item:

1. The shopping cart shall exist.

2. The selected shopping cart item shall exist.

3. The customer shall be authorized to modify the shopping cart.

4. The requested quantity shall be greater than zero.

5. The requested quantity shall not exceed the maximum quantity permitted by the restaurant.

6. The selected menu item shall remain active and available for ordering.

7. The latest pricing information shall be successfully retrieved before recalculating the shopping cart.

8. The system shall verify that sufficient inventory is available, where applicable.

9. The system shall validate all applicable pricing, discount, promotional offer, and tax calculation rules.

10. The updated shopping cart shall satisfy all mandatory business rules before the quantity update is completed.

11. The shopping cart shall remain associated with only one restaurant.

12. The system shall successfully persist the updated shopping cart information after completing the quantity update.

---

#### 3.4.3.8 Main Flow

1. The customer opens the shopping cart.

2. The customer selects a shopping cart item.

3. The customer increases or decreases the item quantity.

4. The customer confirms the updated quantity, where confirmation is required.

5. The system validates the shopping cart, selected item, requested quantity, latest pricing information, item availability, and applicable business rules.

6. The system verifies that the requested quantity is within the permitted limits.

7. The system updates the shopping cart item quantity.

8. The system recalculates the item subtotal.

9. The system recalculates applicable taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, discounts, promotional offers, and the grand total.

10. The system updates the shopping cart with the latest pricing information.

11. The system securely persists the updated shopping cart.

12. The system records the Update Item Quantity activity for auditing, analytics, customer behavior analysis, and operational monitoring purposes.

13. The system displays the updated shopping cart with the revised quantity, pricing breakdown, and grand total.

#### 3.4.3.9 Alternate Flows

##### AF-1: Invalid Quantity

1. The customer enters a quantity less than one.

2. The system validates the requested quantity.

3. The system displays an appropriate validation message indicating that the quantity must be greater than zero.

4. The shopping cart shall remain unchanged.

---

##### AF-2: Maximum Quantity Exceeded

1. The customer enters a quantity exceeding the maximum quantity permitted by the restaurant.

- Audit Logging Service

1. The customer shall have access to the QuickBite platform.

2. A shopping cart shall exist.

3. The shopping cart shall contain at least one menu item.

4. The selected shopping cart item shall exist.

5. The customer shall have permission to modify the shopping cart.

6. The shopping cart shall be successfully retrievable.

7. The latest shopping cart information shall be available before processing the removal request.

#### 3.4.4.4 Trigger

The Remove Item from Cart functionality shall be initiated when the customer selects the **Remove**, **Delete**, or equivalent action for a shopping cart item within the QuickBite platform.

11. Removing a shopping cart item shall not create, modify, or cancel any order.

12. No payment transaction shall be initiated as a result of removing a shopping cart item.

2. The customer shall be authenticated.

3. At least one saved shopping cart shall exist for the customer.

4. The selected saved shopping cart shall be retrievable.

5. The customer shall have permission to access the selected saved shopping cart.

6. The system shall be able to retrieve the latest restaurant, menu, pricing, and inventory information.

7. The shopping cart shall not currently be locked by an active checkout or payment process.

#### 3.4.8.1 Description

7. The coupon shall be active and within its validity period.

### 3.4.12 Calculate Item Total

#### 3.4.12.1 Description

The Calculate Item Total functionality enables the system to calculate the total amount for each menu item in the shopping cart before checkout. The system shall calculate the item total based on the latest unit price, selected quantity, item variants, add-ons, customizations, applicable item-level discounts, and other configured pricing rules. The calculated item total shall be used as an input for subsequent shopping cart pricing calculations.

4. The selected quantity shall be available.

5. Selected variants and add-ons shall be retrievable.

6. Applicable pricing rules shall be available.

7. Applicable item-level discounts and promotional information shall be retrievable.

8. The latest menu pricing information shall be available.

The Calculate Delivery Charges functionality enables the system to determine the applicable delivery charges for a shopping cart before checkout. The system shall calculate the delivery charges based on the delivery distance, delivery location, restaurant location, delivery zone, order value, delivery partner availability, delivery type, surge pricing, free delivery eligibility, promotional benefits, and other configured delivery pricing policies. The calculated delivery charges shall be included in the final shopping cart total.

#### 3.4.14.2 Actors

**Primary Actor**

- Pricing Service

**Supporting Actors**

- Shopping Cart Service
- Delivery Service
- Restaurant Service
- Location Service
- Promotion Service
- Audit Logging Service

#### 3.4.14.3 Preconditions

The following preconditions shall be satisfied before calculating delivery charges:

1. A shopping cart shall exist.

2. The shopping cart shall contain one or more menu items.

3. The restaurant location shall be available.

4. The customer delivery location shall be available.

5. The delivery distance shall be calculable.

6. Applicable delivery pricing policies shall be retrievable.

7. Delivery service availability shall be verified.

8. The latest shopping cart subtotal shall be available.

#### 3.4.14.4 Trigger

The Calculate Delivery Charges functionality shall be initiated automatically whenever the shopping cart is created, modified, restored, the delivery address changes, or whenever pricing-related information changes before checkout.

#### 3.4.14.5 Input Fields

| Field Name | Description | Mandatory |
|------------|-------------|-----------|
| Shopping Cart ID | Unique identifier of the shopping cart. | Yes |
| Restaurant Location | Restaurant location used for delivery calculation. | Yes |
| Delivery Address | Customer delivery address. | Yes |
| Delivery Distance | Distance between restaurant and delivery address. | Yes |
| Delivery Type | Standard, Scheduled, Express, or other supported delivery type. | Yes |
| Applicable Delivery Pricing Rules | Configured delivery pricing policies. | Yes |

#### 3.4.14.6 Business Rules

1. The system shall calculate delivery charges using the latest approved delivery pricing policies.

2. Delivery charges shall be determined based on the restaurant location, customer delivery location, delivery distance, delivery zone, delivery type, order value, delivery partner availability, surge pricing, and other configured delivery pricing rules.

3. Different delivery types, including Standard Delivery, Scheduled Delivery, Express Delivery, and other supported delivery options, may have different delivery pricing structures.

4. The system shall determine delivery distance using the platform's approved routing and distance calculation mechanism.

5. Free delivery shall be applied only when the shopping cart satisfies the configured eligibility criteria, including promotional campaigns, coupon benefits, subscription plans, loyalty programs, minimum order value, or other applicable business rules.

6. Surge pricing shall be applied only during configured operational periods based on demand, weather conditions, traffic conditions, delivery partner availability, or other configured surge pricing policies.

7. Delivery charges shall be recalculated whenever the delivery address, restaurant, shopping cart contents, delivery type, promotional offers, or applicable pricing policies change.

8. Delivery charges shall be included in the final shopping cart total before checkout.

9. Delivery charge calculation shall not reserve a delivery partner, confirm delivery availability, create an order, or initiate a payment transaction.

10. The calculated delivery charges shall comply with the platform's configured precision and rounding policies.

11. Delivery pricing shall support restaurant-specific delivery pricing policies, platform-wide pricing policies, and location-based pricing rules.

12. Every delivery charge calculation shall be recorded for auditing, pricing analysis, delivery analytics, reporting, and operational monitoring purposes.

---

#### 3.4.14.7 Validations

The system shall perform the following validations before calculating delivery charges:

1. The shopping cart shall exist.

2. The shopping cart shall contain one or more menu items.

3. The restaurant location shall be available.

4. The customer delivery address shall be valid and serviceable.

5. The delivery distance shall be successfully calculated.

6. The applicable delivery zone shall be identified.

7. The requested delivery type shall be valid and supported.

8. Delivery service shall be available for the selected restaurant and delivery location.

9. Applicable delivery pricing rules shall be successfully retrieved.

10. Applicable promotional offers, free delivery benefits, loyalty benefits, or subscription benefits shall be validated.

11. The calculated delivery charges shall comply with configured pricing, precision, and rounding policies.

12. All mandatory platform security validations shall be successfully completed.

---

#### 3.4.14.8 Main Flow

1. The system detects a shopping cart event requiring delivery charge recalculation.

2. The system retrieves the restaurant location and customer delivery address.

3. The system calculates the delivery distance and identifies the applicable delivery zone.

4. The system validates the requested delivery type and delivery service availability.

5. The system retrieves the applicable delivery pricing policies.

6. The system validates promotional offers, free delivery eligibility, loyalty benefits, subscription benefits, and other configured pricing rules.

7. The system calculates the applicable delivery charges.

8. The system applies configured precision and rounding rules.

9. The calculated delivery charges are added to the shopping cart pricing information.

10. The system recalculates the shopping cart grand total.

11. The system records the delivery charge calculation for auditing, pricing analysis, delivery analytics, reporting, and operational monitoring purposes.

12. The updated delivery charges are displayed as part of the shopping cart summary.

#### 3.4.14.9 Alternate Flows

##### AF-1: Free Delivery Eligible

1. The system evaluates the shopping cart against the configured free delivery criteria.

2. The shopping cart satisfies one or more free delivery conditions.

3. The delivery charges shall be calculated as zero.

4. The shopping cart shall display the applicable free delivery benefit.

---

##### AF-2: Delivery Address Updated

1. The customer changes the delivery address.

2. The system recalculates the delivery distance and delivery zone.

3. The applicable delivery pricing policies are retrieved.

4. The delivery charges are recalculated.

5. The updated delivery charges shall be displayed.

---

##### AF-3: Surge Pricing Applicable

1. The system detects an active surge pricing period.

2. The applicable surge pricing rules are retrieved.

3. The delivery charges are recalculated by applying the configured surge pricing policy.

4. The updated delivery charges shall be displayed to the customer.

---

##### AF-4: Delivery Service Not Available

1. The system validates delivery availability.

2. The selected delivery address is outside the supported delivery area or delivery service is temporarily unavailable.

3. Delivery charge calculation is aborted.

4. The customer shall be informed that delivery is currently unavailable for the selected address.

---

##### AF-5: Delivery Pricing Rules Not Available

1. The system attempts to retrieve the applicable delivery pricing policies.

2. The delivery pricing configuration cannot be retrieved.

3. The delivery charge calculation is aborted.

4. The customer shall be informed that delivery charges cannot be calculated at this time.

5. The calculation may be retried after the required pricing information becomes available.

1. An unexpected application, database, or network failure occurs during delivery charge calculation.

2. The system aborts the calculation process.

3. An appropriate error message shall be displayed.

4. No incomplete or inconsistent delivery charge information shall be stored.

5. The calculation may be retried after the issue is resolved.

---

##### Successful Completion

1. The applicable delivery charges shall be successfully calculated.

2. The calculated delivery charges shall be associated with the shopping cart.

3. The shopping cart grand total shall include the calculated delivery charges.

4. The delivery charges shall comply with configured pricing, precision, and rounding policies.

##### Unsuccessful Completion

1. Delivery charges shall not be calculated.

2. Previously validated shopping cart pricing information shall remain unchanged.

3. The customer shall be informed of the reason for the failure.

4. No incomplete or partially calculated delivery charge information shall be stored.

5. No customer profile, restaurant information, inventory data, pricing information, delivery assignment, or order information shall be modified due to the failed calculation.

6. The current shopping cart state shall remain unchanged until a successful delivery charge calculation is completed.

#### 3.4.14.11 Success Response

Upon successful completion of the Calculate Delivery Charges functionality, the system shall:

1. Display the calculated delivery charges applicable to the shopping cart.

2. Display the delivery pricing breakdown, where supported by platform policies.

3. Display the delivery distance and applicable delivery zone, where permitted by platform configuration.

4. Display any applied free delivery benefits, promotional discounts, subscription benefits, loyalty benefits, or surge pricing adjustments.

8. Record the successful Calculate Delivery Charges activity for auditing, pricing analysis, delivery analytics, reporting, and operational monitoring purposes.

9. Ensure that calculating delivery charges does not reserve a delivery partner, confirm delivery availability, create an order, modify an existing order, or initiate a payment transaction.

10. Make the calculated delivery charges available for checkout processing, payment calculation, invoice generation, order summary generation, and customer billing.

11. Provide a secure, accurate, reliable, and responsive delivery charge calculation experience across all supported platforms and devices.

#### 3.4.14.12 Failure Response

If the Calculate Delivery Charges functionality cannot be completed successfully, the system shall:

6. Record the failed Calculate Delivery Charges activity for diagnostics, auditing, pricing analysis, reporting, security monitoring, and operational monitoring purposes, where applicable.

### 3.4.17 Calculate Discount Amount

#### 3.4.17.1 Description

The Calculate Discount Amount functionality enables the system to determine the total discount applicable to the shopping cart before checkout. The system shall calculate discounts based on coupons, promotional campaigns, restaurant offers, platform offers, loyalty rewards, subscription benefits, referral rewards, item-level discounts, cart-level discounts, and other configured pricing policies. The calculated discount amount shall be deducted from the shopping cart according to the platform's pricing rules.

#### 3.4.17.2 Actors

**Primary Actor**

- Pricing Service

**Supporting Actors**

- Shopping Cart Service
- Promotion Service
- Coupon Service
- Loyalty Service
- Subscription Service
- Restaurant Service
- Audit Logging Service

#### 3.4.17.3 Preconditions

The following preconditions shall be satisfied before calculating the discount amount:

1. A shopping cart shall exist.

2. The shopping cart shall contain one or more eligible menu items.

3. The latest shopping cart subtotal shall be available.

4. Applicable promotional campaigns and pricing policies shall be retrievable.

5. Applied coupons, if any, shall already be validated.

6. Customer loyalty and subscription information shall be available, where applicable.

7. Restaurant promotional offers shall be available, where applicable.

8. The latest pricing information shall be available.

#### 3.4.17.4 Trigger

The Calculate Discount Amount functionality shall be initiated automatically whenever the shopping cart is created, modified, restored, coupons are applied or removed, promotional offers change, customer membership changes, loyalty rewards change, or whenever pricing-related information changes before checkout.

#### 3.4.17.5 Input Fields

| Field Name | Description | Mandatory |
|------------|-------------|-----------|
| Shopping Cart ID | Unique identifier of the shopping cart. | Yes |
| Shopping Cart Subtotal | Latest calculated subtotal before discount calculation. | Yes |
| Applied Coupon | Coupon selected by the customer, if applicable. | No |
| Applicable Promotional Offers | Eligible restaurant and platform promotional offers. | Yes |
| Customer Membership Details | Loyalty, subscription, or membership information. | No |
| Applicable Discount Rules | Configured discount calculation policies. | Yes |

#### 3.4.17.6 Business Rules

1. The system shall calculate the discount amount using the latest approved pricing, promotion, and discount policies.

2. Discounts may originate from coupons, promotional campaigns, restaurant offers, platform offers, loyalty rewards, referral programs, subscription benefits, item-level discounts, cart-level discounts, or any combination supported by the platform.

3. The system shall determine discount eligibility based on configured business rules, including order value, eligible menu items, restaurant participation, customer eligibility, promotion validity period, usage limits, and other applicable conditions.

4. Multiple discounts shall be combined only when allowed by the configured discount stacking policy.

5. When multiple eligible discounts exist, the system shall apply the discount combination that complies with the configured business rules and pricing policies.

6. Discounts shall never exceed the maximum discount amount configured for the applicable promotion or pricing policy.

7. The total discount amount shall never exceed the eligible payable amount of the shopping cart.

8. Item-level discounts shall be calculated before cart-level discounts where required by the configured pricing policy.

9. The system shall automatically recalculate discounts whenever the shopping cart contents, subtotal, coupons, promotional offers, customer membership, subscription status, restaurant offers, or pricing policies change.

10. The calculated discount amount shall be deducted from the shopping cart according to the configured pricing sequence before calculating the final payable amount.

11. Discount calculation shall not reserve inventory, assign a delivery partner, create an order, or initiate a payment transaction.

12. Every discount calculation shall be recorded for auditing, pricing analysis, financial reporting, promotion analytics, and operational monitoring purposes.

---

#### 3.4.17.7 Validations

The system shall perform the following validations before calculating the discount amount:

1. The shopping cart shall exist.

2. The shopping cart shall contain one or more eligible menu items.

3. The latest shopping cart subtotal shall be successfully calculated.

4. Applied coupons shall be validated for eligibility, validity period, usage limits, and redemption status.

5. Applicable promotional campaigns shall be active and available.

6. Customer eligibility for loyalty rewards, subscription benefits, membership benefits, referral rewards, or other promotional programs shall be validated where applicable.

7. Restaurant participation in applicable promotional campaigns shall be validated.

8. The calculated discount amount shall comply with configured minimum order value, maximum discount amount, discount percentage, and stacking policies.

9. Duplicate or conflicting discounts shall be prevented according to configured pricing rules.

10. All mandatory business rules shall be validated before completing discount calculation.

11. All mandatory platform security validations shall be successfully completed.

12. The calculated discount amount shall be compatible with the latest shopping cart pricing information.

---

#### 3.4.17.8 Main Flow

1. The system detects a shopping cart event requiring discount recalculation.

2. The system retrieves the latest shopping cart subtotal.

3. The system retrieves all applicable coupons, promotional campaigns, restaurant offers, platform offers, loyalty rewards, subscription benefits, and referral rewards.

4. The system validates customer eligibility for each applicable discount.

5. The system validates promotion validity periods, usage limits, minimum order values, and other configured eligibility criteria.

6. The system determines the applicable discount calculation sequence according to configured pricing policies.

7. The system calculates item-level discounts.

8. The system calculates cart-level discounts.

9. The system applies discount stacking rules, maximum discount limits, and other configured pricing policies.

10. The system calculates the final discount amount.

11. The calculated discount amount is applied to the shopping cart pricing information.

12. The system recalculates the shopping cart payable amount and grand total.

13. The system records the discount calculation for auditing, pricing analysis, financial reporting, promotion analytics, and operational monitoring purposes.

14. The updated discount amount is displayed as part of the shopping cart summary.

### 3.4.18 Calculate Grand Total

#### 3.4.18.1 Description

The Calculate Grand Total functionality enables the system to calculate the final payable amount of the shopping cart before checkout. The system shall aggregate all pricing components, including item subtotal, taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, discounts, promotional benefits, and other applicable charges to determine the final grand total. The calculated grand total shall represent the amount payable by the customer during checkout.

#### 3.4.18.2 Actors

**Primary Actor**

- Pricing Service

**Supporting Actors**

- Shopping Cart Service
- Tax Calculation Service
- Delivery Service
- Promotion Service
- Payment Service
- Audit Logging Service

#### 3.4.18.3 Preconditions

The following preconditions shall be satisfied before calculating the grand total:

1. A shopping cart shall exist.

2. The shopping cart shall contain one or more menu items.

3. The shopping cart subtotal shall be successfully calculated.

4. Applicable taxes shall be successfully calculated.

5. Applicable delivery charges shall be successfully calculated.

6. Applicable packaging charges shall be successfully calculated.

7. Applicable platform fees shall be successfully calculated.

8. Applicable discounts and promotional benefits shall be successfully calculated.

9. All pricing components required for checkout shall be available.

#### 3.4.18.4 Trigger

The Calculate Grand Total functionality shall be initiated automatically whenever any shopping cart pricing component changes, including shopping cart contents, item quantities, taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, discounts, promotional offers, or any pricing-related information before checkout.

#### 3.4.18.5 Input Fields

| Field Name | Description | Mandatory |
|------------|-------------|-----------|
| Shopping Cart ID | Unique identifier of the shopping cart. | Yes |
| Item Subtotal | Total price of all menu items before additional calculations. | Yes |
| Tax Amount | Total applicable taxes. | Yes |
| Delivery Charges | Applicable delivery charges. | Yes |
| Packaging Charges | Applicable packaging charges. | Yes |
| Platform Fee | Applicable platform service fee. | Yes |
| Discount Amount | Total applicable discounts. | Yes |
| Other Applicable Charges | Any additional configured pricing components. | No |

#### 3.4.18.6 Business Rules

1. The system shall calculate the grand total using the latest approved pricing policies and all validated pricing components.

2. The grand total shall be calculated by aggregating the shopping cart subtotal, applicable taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, and other applicable charges, and then deducting all eligible discounts according to the configured pricing sequence.

3. The calculation sequence shall follow the platform's configured pricing workflow to ensure consistent and accurate pricing.

4. Only validated pricing components shall participate in the grand total calculation.

5. The system shall support configurable pricing components, allowing future pricing elements to be included without affecting existing pricing calculations.

6. The grand total shall never be a negative value. If the calculated amount is less than zero, the payable amount shall be set to zero unless otherwise configured by business policies.

7. All pricing components shall comply with the platform's configured precision, rounding, minimum payable amount, and maximum payable amount policies.

8. The grand total shall be automatically recalculated whenever any pricing component changes, including shopping cart contents, taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, discounts, promotional offers, or applicable pricing policies.

9. The calculated grand total shall represent the final payable amount presented to the customer before checkout.

10. Grand total calculation shall not reserve inventory, assign a delivery partner, create an order, authorize payment, or initiate a payment transaction.

11. The calculated grand total shall be available for checkout processing, payment processing, invoice generation, order confirmation, financial reporting, and customer billing.

12. Every grand total calculation shall be recorded for auditing, pricing analysis, financial reporting, analytics, compliance, and operational monitoring purposes.

4. All mandatory pricing components shall be successfully calculated.

1. The system shall automatically initiate the cart merge process after successful customer authentication when both a guest cart and a customer cart exist.

2. The guest shopping cart and the customer shopping cart shall be merged according to the configured cart merge policies.

3. If identical menu items with the same variations and customizations exist in both carts, the system shall merge them by applying the configured quantity merge rules.

4. If configured merge policies prohibit quantity merging, duplicate items shall remain as separate cart entries.

5. Before merging, the system shall validate the availability of every cart item against the latest restaurant inventory.

6. Items that are unavailable, discontinued, inactive, or no longer offered by the restaurant shall be handled according to the configured inventory policies.

7. The system shall recalculate pricing, discounts, taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, promotional offers, and grand total after the merge is completed.

8. Coupons or promotional offers that are no longer valid after the merge shall be automatically removed, and the customer shall be informed.

9. If the guest cart and customer cart belong to different restaurants, the system shall resolve the conflict according to configured platform business policies.

10. The merged shopping cart shall comply with all applicable inventory, pricing, promotional, restaurant, and checkout business rules.

11. The guest shopping cart shall be invalidated or deleted after a successful merge in accordance with configured data retention policies.

12. Every cart merge activity shall be recorded for auditing, customer activity tracking, reporting, compliance, operational monitoring, and business analytics purposes.

### 3.4.25 Persist Shopping Cart

#### 3.4.25.1 Description

The Persist Shopping Cart functionality enables the system to securely save and maintain the shopping cart state so that customers can continue their shopping experience across sessions, devices, and application restarts. The system shall automatically persist shopping cart data whenever modifications occur and shall restore the latest valid shopping cart when the customer resumes the session. Shopping cart persistence shall ensure data integrity, consistency, reliability, and compliance with configured data retention and security policies.

#### 3.4.25.2 Actors

**Primary Actor**

- Shopping Cart Service

**Supporting Actors**

- Customer Service
- Authentication Service
- Database Service
- Cache Service
- Checkout Service
- Audit Logging Service

#### 3.4.25.3 Preconditions

The following preconditions shall be satisfied before persisting the shopping cart:

1. A shopping cart shall exist.

2. The shopping cart shall contain valid shopping cart data.

3. The customer session or guest session shall be active.

4. The shopping cart shall pass mandatory validation checks.

5. Database connectivity shall be available.

6. Applicable shopping cart persistence policies shall be retrievable.

7. The authenticated customer account or guest session shall be identifiable.

8. Required storage services shall be operational.

#### 3.4.25.4 Trigger

The Persist Shopping Cart functionality shall be initiated automatically whenever the shopping cart is created, updated, restored, merged, validated, refreshed, customer authentication status changes, checkout is initiated, or any shopping cart data modification occurs.

#### 3.4.25.5 Input Fields

| Field Name | Description | Mandatory |
|------------|-------------|-----------|
| Shopping Cart ID | Unique identifier of the shopping cart. | Yes |
| Customer ID / Guest Session ID | Identifier of the authenticated customer or guest session. | Yes |
| Shopping Cart Data | Complete shopping cart information to be persisted. | Yes |
| Shopping Cart Version | Current version of the shopping cart used for concurrency management. | Yes |
| Persistence Rules | Configured shopping cart persistence policies. | Yes |
| Retention Policy | Configured shopping cart data retention policy. | Yes |
| Session Information | Current customer or guest session information. | Yes |

#### 3.4.25.6 Business Rules

1. The system shall automatically persist the shopping cart whenever the shopping cart is created, modified, restored, merged, or validated.

2. The system shall support shopping cart persistence for both authenticated customers and guest users.

3. The persisted shopping cart shall include all shopping cart items, quantities, customizations, pricing information, applied discounts, promotional offers, coupons, delivery preferences, and other shopping cart metadata required for restoration.

4. The system shall maintain version control for shopping cart data to prevent concurrent update conflicts.

5. Shopping cart persistence shall comply with configured data retention and expiration policies.

6. Expired shopping carts may be automatically archived or deleted according to configured retention policies.

7. Whenever a persisted shopping cart is restored, the system shall revalidate inventory availability, pricing, promotional offers, taxes, delivery charges, packaging charges, platform fees, and applicable business rules.

8. The system shall securely store shopping cart information using approved security mechanisms, including encryption where required by platform security policies.

9. Shopping cart persistence shall not create an order, reserve inventory permanently, assign a delivery partner, authorize payment, or initiate a payment transaction.

10. The system shall support cross-device shopping cart synchronization for authenticated customers according to configured synchronization policies.

11. Any modification to the persisted shopping cart shall replace or version the previous persisted state according to configured concurrency policies.

12. Every shopping cart persistence activity shall be recorded for auditing, customer activity tracking, reporting, compliance, operational monitoring, and business analytics purposes.

---

The system shall perform the following validations before persisting the shopping cart:

1. The shopping cart shall exist.

2. The shopping cart shall contain valid shopping cart data.

3. Every shopping cart item shall satisfy mandatory validation requirements.

4. The customer account or guest session shall be valid.

5. The shopping cart version shall be valid for persistence.

8. The shopping cart data shall comply with configured data integrity requirements.

9. Mandatory security validations shall be successfully completed before persistence.

10. All mandatory business validations shall be successfully completed.

11. All mandatory platform security validations shall be completed.

12. The persisted shopping cart shall be internally consistent with the latest validated shopping cart information.
