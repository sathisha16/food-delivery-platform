# 1. Purpose

The Project Roadmap defines the strategic execution plan for the QuickBite project from product discovery through production deployment and future product evolution.

It provides a high-level view of project phases, major deliverables, milestones and implementation priorities, ensuring all development activities remain aligned with the overall product vision.

Unlike sprint planning or task management, this roadmap focuses on long-term project progression and engineering milestones.

---

# 2. Product Vision

QuickBite aims to become a scalable, secure and maintainable food delivery platform built using enterprise software engineering practices.

The roadmap is designed to guide the project through every stage of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC), ensuring controlled delivery, technical excellence and long-term maintainability.

# 7. Success Criteria

The roadmap shall be considered successfully executed when:

- All planned deliverables are completed.
- Functional and non-functional requirements are implemented.
- Architecture complies with approved engineering standards.
- Quality assurance activities are successfully completed.
- Production deployment is completed successfully.
- Version 1.0 is released.

# 8. Roadmap Governance

The following governance rules apply:

- The roadmap shall reflect the overall strategic direction of the project.
- Major milestones shall only be updated after successful completion.
- Changes to roadmap phases require project review.
- New phases may be appended as the product evolves.
- Completed phases shall remain unchanged to preserve project history.
